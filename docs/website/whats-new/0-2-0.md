# What's new in 0.2.0

**Included in Free.** Product Roadmap has no paid version.

- **My votes.** Add `/account/roadmap-votes` to your menu. A signed-in member sees what they voted for and the ideas they sent, and can take every vote back with a second click.
- **Ideas at a glance.** Ideas opens with how many ideas are waiting, items per stage and votes this week. Waiting ideas have Find and show 25 at a time.
- **The board inside post text.** The optional Roadmap block places the board, with voting, in the middle of a post. It needs the trusted install described in the setup guide.
- **Safer server calls.** The board and Suggest page check settings inside EmDash, with no web request.

Copy the site kit again after you update.
