A public roadmap for any EmDash site. Visitors see what you are considering, building and have shipped. They can vote, and they can send an idea that waits for you to approve it.

## What you get

- **A board with stages you name.** Start with Under review, Planned, In progress and Done. Add, hide, rename or reorder them.
- **Voting.** One vote per person per item. Click again to take it back.
- **Suggestions with approval.** An idea is saved as a draft. You approve it onto the board or reject it.
- **Guests or members.** You choose who can vote and suggest. Guests add a name. Written ideas always wait for review.
- **A changelog.** A second collection with a list page and a page per release.
- **Comments.** The item page uses EmDash's own comments. Nothing extra to moderate.
- **Merge.** Fold a duplicate into the item you keep. Votes move across, one per person.

## How it fits together

The plugin is the engine: routes, storage and the admin screens. An EmDash plugin cannot add pages to your site, so the public pages ship as a site kit you copy in once. See Installation.

The hourly email needs a mail provider. The dashboard card still shows how many ideas are waiting when mail is off.
