# What's new in 0.4.2

**Included in Free.** Product Roadmap has no paid version. No new permission.

- **Authors and Editors can set an item's stage.** The Stage panel on a roadmap item now works for Editors on any item and for Authors on the items they wrote. Before, only site admins could use it. Contributors and Subscribers still do not see it. See [Items and stages](../using/items-and-stages.md).
- **Every waiting idea is reachable.** Ideas lists waiting ideas 25 at a time with **Previous** and **Next**, and **Ideas waiting** counts all of them. Before, only the newest 100 could be decided from the list. **Find** looks through 100 waiting ideas at a time; **Next** looks through older ones. See [Suggestions](../using/suggestions.md).
- **Members see all the ideas they sent.** The Roadmap votes page lists 50 ideas at a time with **Show more ideas**. Before, it stopped at 50.
- **Needs EmDash 1.2.0 or later.** The registry now says so.

After you update, copy the site kit with tag `v0.4.2`. The Roadmap votes page and the wording file changed.
