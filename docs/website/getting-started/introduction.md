# Introduction

**Included in Free.** Product Roadmap has no paid version.

![The public roadmap board with four stage columns and a vote button on each item](../images/board.webp)

Product Roadmap shows your visitors what you are considering, building and have shipped. They can vote on what matters to them and suggest ideas of their own. You decide what moves forward.

It fits any EmDash site: a product or SaaS roadmap, "what should we write about next" for a blog, services clients ask for, or a project plan with status.

## What you can do

- Show a public board with one column per stage. It starts with Under review, Planned, In progress and Done.
- Name, reorder, add and hide stages to suit your site.
- Let visitors vote once per item. They click again to take the vote back.
- Choose who can take part: anyone, or only signed-in members.
- Accept suggested ideas. Every idea waits for your approval before it reaches the board.
- Let people comment on an item with EmDash's own comments.
- Merge a duplicate idea into the item you keep. Votes move across, one per person.
- Publish a changelog with a list page and a page for each release.
- Get one short email an hour when ideas are waiting, plus a card on your EmDash dashboard.
- Filter the board by category and sort it by Most votes or Newest.

## How it fits together

Product Roadmap has two parts:

| Part | What it does | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The plugin | Stores votes and ideas, and gives you the Ideas and Settings screens in admin | Installed from the EmDash registry |
| The site kit | Draws the board, item pages, suggest form and changelog on your site | A few files your developer copies into the site once |

EmDash plugins from the registry run in a safe sandbox and cannot add pages to your site by themselves. That is why the visible part comes as a site kit. After your developer copies it, you manage everything from admin.
