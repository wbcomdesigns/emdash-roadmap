# What's new in 0.1.3

**Included in Free.** Product Roadmap has no paid version.

## For site owners

- **Your edits to an idea are never overwritten.** Tidy a suggestion in the content screens and publish it there; it leaves the Ideas list by itself. Approve no longer puts the visitor's original text back over your edits, and it never publishes an edited draft you have not seen.
- **At least one stage stays on the board.** Settings will not hide the last shown stage.
- **Approved ideas always reach the board.** New and approved ideas go to the first stage that is shown, so hiding your first stage no longer hides new items.
- **User guides** cover install, the board, items and stages, voting, suggestions, merging, the changelog, every setting, FAQ and troubleshooting.

## For developers

- Plugin update only; the site kit is unchanged from 0.1.2.
