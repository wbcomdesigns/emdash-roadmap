# Your first steps

**Included in Free.** Product Roadmap has no paid version.

This walks through a working board from start to finish: one item, one vote, one suggested idea and one release note.

## 1. Check your settings

1. In admin, open **Roadmap**, then **Settings**.
2. Open the **Who can take part** tab. Leave **Allow suggestions** on and **Who can vote and suggest** on **Anyone** for now. Choose **Save**.
3. Open the **Stages** tab. Keep Under review, Planned, In progress and Done, or rename them. See [Items and stages](../using/items-and-stages.md).

## 2. Add your first item

1. In admin, open the **Roadmap** content type and add a new entry.
2. Fill in the **Title** and the **Description**. The description is shown on the item's page.
3. Pick a category if you made any.
4. Open the **Stage** panel in the editor sidebar, pick a stage, and choose **Save stage**.
5. Publish the entry.

Only published items appear on the board.

## 3. See it as a visitor

Open `/roadmap/` in a private window. Your item sits in the column for its stage, with a **Vote** button and a count. Choose **Vote**. The button changes to **Voted** and the count goes up by one. Choose it again to take the vote back.

## 4. Send an idea

1. On the board, choose **Suggest an idea**.
2. Enter a **Title**, a **Description** and **Your name**, then choose **Send idea**.
3. You see "Thanks. Your idea is waiting for review."

## 5. Approve the idea

1. In admin, open **Roadmap**, then **Ideas**. The idea is in the table.
2. In its row, choose **Decide**, then **Approve**.
3. The idea is published in your first stage, **Under review** by default. Reload the board to see it.

See [Suggestions and the Ideas queue](../using/suggestions.md) for what **Reject** does.

## 6. Add a release note

1. In admin, open the **Changelog** content type and add an entry with a **Title**, a **Summary** and a **Released** date.
2. Publish it.
3. Open `/changelog/`. The release is listed under its month.
